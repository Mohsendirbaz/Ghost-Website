1. **Prioritize clarity and simplicity**: remove unnecessary elements, reduce cognitive load, and guide users directly toward their goals.
2. **Establish strong visual hierarchy and consistency**: use typography, spacing, contrast, and repeatable patterns to make interfaces predictable and scannable.
3. **Provide clear feedback and accessible interactions**: confirm user actions, show loading/error states, and ensure keyboard, contrast, and usability standards are met.
4. **Design for performance and conversion across devices**: optimize forms, support mobile-first behavior, and make primary actions obvious and easy to complete.
5. **Standardize and validate continuously**: maintain a documented design system and verify decisions through usability testing, analytics, and iterative refinement.
