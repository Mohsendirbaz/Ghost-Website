## TONE DEFINITIONS

**Academic (A)**: Scholarly, balanced, intellectually rigorous, citation-heavy, theoretical frameworks
**Technical (T)**: Precise, methodological, empirically grounded, quantitative analysis, equations/models
**General (G)**: Accessible, narrative-driven, morally engaged, broader audience appeal
**Policy (P)**: Authoritative, action-oriented, pragmatic, prescriptive, implementation-focused

**Tone Notation**: [A:X% | T:Y% | G:Z% | P:W%] where X+Y+Z+W = 100%

## QUALITY CONTROL CHECKPOINTS

1. **Argument Structure (Toulmin)**:
    - Clear claim stated upfront
    - Sufficient grounds (data/evidence)
    - Explicit warrant connecting evidence to claim

2. **Cognitive Depth (Bloom’s)**:
    - Knowledge: Facts established
    - Comprehension: Concepts explained
    - Application: Used in new contexts
    - Analysis: Connections drawn
    - Synthesis: Elements combined
    - Evaluation: Positions justified

    - Clear section-to-section progression

    - No orphaned concepts
    - Consistent terminology

    - Sentence complexity matches audience
