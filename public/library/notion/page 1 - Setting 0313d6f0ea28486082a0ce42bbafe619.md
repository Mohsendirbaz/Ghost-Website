- *Chicago's Cold Winter**
Douglas Street stretches through the South Side, a corridor of memory and survival. The wind cuts through from the lake, the kind that makes your bones ache even through layers. This isn't the Chicago of downtown skylines and tourist snapshots - this is the Chicago where winter isn't picturesque, it's a trial.
The temperature hovers in the teens. Ice forms in the cracks of sidewalks. Breath hangs visible in the air like small ghosts.
