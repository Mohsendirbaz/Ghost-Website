TARGET: Replace 20-30% of repeated noun phrases with pronouns where unambiguous

CURRENT STATE: Pronominal underuse - relies on full noun phrase repetition
GOAL: Improve entity-based coherence while maintaining clarity

EXAMPLE TRANSFORMATIONS:

**Before:**
"The void fraction framework quantifies the absence of intact two-parent families. The void fraction framework measures..."

**After:**
"The void fraction framework quantifies the absence of intact two-parent families. It measures..."

**Before:**
"The forty-point-five percent national out-of-wedlock birth rate represents... The forty-point-five percent figure illustrates..."

**After:**
"The forty-point-five percent national out-of-wedlock birth rate represents... This figure illustrates..."

PRONOUN TYPES TO DEPLOY:
- "It" for singular technical terms (void fraction framework)
- "This/These" for statistical references (this rate, these patterns)
- "They" for plural entities (convicts, children)

CAUTION ZONES:
- Avoid ambiguous pronoun references
- Maintain technical precision where needed
- Keep first mention as full noun phrase
- Use pronouns only for entities recently mentioned (within 2-3 sentences)

VALIDATION: Entity grid analysis should show improved S→S transitions
