**MACRO FACTS:**
- General models: 70B-1.7T parameters, $10-100/M tokens API cost
- Specialized models: 1B-20B parameters, $0.10-$5/M tokens, 10-100x faster
- Task performance: specialized models often match/exceed general on narrow tasks
- Privacy advantage: on-premise specialized models avoid data sharing with cloud providers
