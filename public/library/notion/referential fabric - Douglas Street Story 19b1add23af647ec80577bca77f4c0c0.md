## The Referential Fabric Concept
Each page:
- **Inherits context** from its parent (cascading meaning)
- **Adds specificity** at its level (depth = detail)
- **Cross-references** other pages through metadata annotations
- **Builds thematic connections** across the hierarchy
This demonstrates Asana's capability as a **hierarchical referential database** where:
- Subtask depth = narrative depth
- Parent-child relationships = contextual inheritance
- Custom fields (planned) = cross-entity connective tissue
- Task notes = rich content containers
- Metadata tags = semantic linking
The 6-level limit becomes a constraint that forces precision - like a sonnet or haiku forces poetic economy.
