- *CHAPTER 9: Open vs Closed AI - The Central Governance Challenge**
**Content Summary:**
Examines the fundamental tension between open-source AI models and closed proprietary systems. Analyzes how governance choices around model access determine whether AI capabilities concentrate among incumbents or distribute to new entrants. Parallel to climate book's "Entrants vs Incumbents" chapter.
