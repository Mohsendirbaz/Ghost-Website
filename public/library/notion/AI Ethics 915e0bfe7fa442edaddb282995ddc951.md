## Project Description

- *BOOK PROJECT: Dishonest AI is Dangerous AI - Accountability, Equity, and Technical Transparency in Artificial Intelligence**
Why America should not be trusted to lead the efforts on AI Ethics.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
**📖 PROJECT OVERVIEW**
This Asana project serves as the complete structural map for "Dishonest AI is Dangerous AI," a 400-500 page academic/policy book examining AI safety, transparency, and equity through integrated technical, policy, and social justice lenses.
**DUAL FUNCTION OF THIS PROJECT MAP:**
1️⃣ **READER NAVIGATION SYSTEM**
This project maps SEVEN distinct reading paths through the book, allowing different reader types to extract maximum value based on their backgrounds and needs: - AI Policy Maker / Regulator Path - AI/ML Engineer / Research Scientist Path - AI Ethics Researcher / Academic Path - CS/AI Graduate Student Path - Tech-Literate Public / Concerned Citizens Path - Impacted Communities / Workers Path - Civil Society / Labor Organizer Path
2️⃣ **AUTHORIAL FINE-TUNING INSTRUMENT**
Each chapter task includes reader-type tags (CRITICAL/IMPORTANT/OPTIONAL/SKIP) that guide: - Depth calibration for mixed audiences - Page count allocation decisions - Cross-reference strategy - Technical vs accessible content balance
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
**🎯 BOOK MISSION**
This book prosecutes algorithmic dishonesty as a systemic threat to:
- Democratic governance and public trust
- Economic equity and labor dignity
- Technical safety and reliability
- Environmental sustainability (AI's carbon footprint)
- Institutional accountability
Just as "Prosecuting Inequity" examined climate investment through equity, capacity, and resource allocation failures, this book examines AI deployment through transparency deficits, monopoly concentration, and benefit capture by incumbent tech firms.
The book **IS Arguing:**
- ✅ The absence of legitimate AI governance enables self-dealing patterns
- ✅ Proposed governance frameworks reproduce capture patterns from other domains
- ✅ Industry self-regulation masquerades as governance
- ✅ Why attempts at governance keep failing: they don't address structural conflicts
