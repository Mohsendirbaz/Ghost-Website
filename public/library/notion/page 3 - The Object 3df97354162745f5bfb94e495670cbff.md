- *Infant Shoes - "Slightly Used"**
A pair of infant shoes. Tiny. The kind for a baby who's just learning to stand, who wobbles and grabs at furniture edges. Soft sole, probably size 2 or 3. They're clean - someone cared enough to clean them before this moment.
The label: "Slightly Used"
Such careful language. Slightly. As if minimizing could soften the story these shoes carry. As if "gently worn" or "barely broken in" would make what they represent less final.
They're too small to have been worn much. A few months maybe. Scuff marks on the toe where a baby kicked against a high chair or tested the ground. The laces still tied in the careful way a parent ties them, not wanting them to come loose.
