## A Family Tradition Continuing for Generations

### 1898: Friedrich Trump and "Mining the Miners"

In 1898, Friedrich Trump, Donald Trump's grandfather, made a shrewd decision: **Instead of digging for gold, extract money from the miners.**

Gwenda Blair, the family biographer, wrote: **"He was mining the miners."**

**The business model was straightforward:**

- Miners arrive in Yukon seeking fortune
- They require food, drink, lodging, and "entertainment"
- Friedrich provides all of these services
- **He builds wealth with high profit margins (alcohol + prostitution) while 96% of miners find no gold**

**Result:** In just 3 years, Friedrich returned with the equivalent of $582,000 today - **without ever picking up a shovel.**
