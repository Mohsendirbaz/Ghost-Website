## Readiness Assessment Framework

### Readiness Dimensions (each scored 0-10)

1. **Technical Maturity** - Core technologies exist and work reliably
2. **Institutional Infrastructure** - Organizations/roles needed exist or are easily created
3. **Regulatory Clarity** - Legal framework supports or doesn't block implementation
4. **Economic Viability** - Business model exists, costs are acceptable
5. **Human Capital** - Skilled people available, training exists
6. **Tooling & Standards** - Reference implementations, APIs, protocols exist
7. **Adoption Incentives** - Clear value proposition, early adopters identified
8. **Integration Readiness** - Fits into existing workflows/systems

**Overall Readiness Score** = Minimum of 8 dimensions (bottleneck principle)

### Gap Taxonomy

**Technical Gaps:**
- T1: Core algorithms don't exist
- T2: Algorithms exist but unreliable
- T3: Scalability problems
- T4: Interoperability issues
- T5: Security/privacy unsolved

**Institutional Gaps:**
- I1: No organizational precedent
- I2: Role definitions unclear
- I3: Governance structure untested
- I4: Accountability mechanisms missing
- I5: Cross-organization coordination needed

**Regulatory Gaps:**
- R1: Legally prohibited or gray area
- R2: Liability unclear
- R3: Compliance costs prohibitive
- R4: International harmonization needed
- R5: Standards bodies don't exist

**Economic Gaps:**
- E1: No viable business model
- E2: Costs exceed benefits for early adopters
- E3: Network effects prevent bootstrapping
- E4: Funding sources unclear
- E5: Market structure misaligned with goals

**Human Capital Gaps:**
- H1: Required expertise doesn't exist
- H2: Expertise exists but scarce
- H3: Training programs needed
- H4: Cultural change required
- H5: Coordination skills lacking

**Tooling Gaps:**
- O1: No reference implementation
- O2: Implementation exists but proprietary
- O3: Standards fragmented
- O4: APIs immature
- O5: Documentation poor

**Adoption Gaps:**
- A1: Value proposition unclear
- A2: Switching costs too high
- A3: No champion organizations
- A4: Competitive dynamics work against adoption
- A5: User experience inadequate

**Integration Gaps:**
- G1: Requires complete workflow redesign
- G2: Legacy system conflicts
- G3: Data format incompatibilities
- G4: Organizational structure mismatch
- G5: Timing/latency constraints

## RISK MITIGATION STRATEGIES

### Technical Risks

**Risk:** Verifier AI not reliably better than Proposer AI  
**Mitigation:** Invest in adversarial training, mechanistic interpretability; use ensemble verifiers; fall back to human verification for high-stakes

**Risk:** Real-time constitutional verification too slow  
**Mitigation:** Develop "fast path" for low-stakes decisions; optimize algorithms; use hierarchical verification (quick first pass, deep check for escalations)

**Risk:** Formal verification of constitutional properties intractable  
**Mitigation:** Focus on empirical testing, statistical guarantees; accept some risk; insurance/liability models

### Institutional Risks

**Risk:** Organizations reject constitutional overhead  
**Mitigation:** Prove ROI in high-stakes cases first; make tools seamless; create regulatory/insurance incentives; competitive pressure (late adopters lose trust)

**Risk:** Separation of powers collapses under pressure  
**Mitigation:** Legal protections; independent budgets; transparency (violations are public); whistleblower protections

**Risk:** Constitutional roles attract wrong people (bureaucrats, not truth-seekers)  
**Mitigation:** Careful selection; performance metrics tied to blind-spot discovery, not just compliance; rotation policies

### Regulatory Risks

**Risk:** Regulatory fragmentation (50 states, international)  
**Mitigation:** Start with federal framework; interstate compacts; work with international bodies (EU, UN); economic incentives for adoption

**Risk:** Liability creates perverse incentives  
**Mitigation:** Safe harbor for constitutional compliance; shift liability to willful violations; insurance products; clear standards

### Economic Risks

**Risk:** Constitutional systems too expensive  
**Mitigation:** Focus on high-stakes domains where costs are justified; develop economies of scale; shared infrastructure; open source

**Risk:** No sustainable business model  
**Mitigation:** Mix of public funding (elections, intelligence, healthcare), private (finance, legal), consortium (industry standards)

### Cultural Risks

**Risk:** Truth-skeptical culture rejects constitutional constraints  
**Mitigation:** Frame as error reduction, not censorship; transparency in corrections; celebrate blind-spot discovery; bipartisan champions

**Risk:** Expertise-skeptical populism  
**Mitigation:** Emphasize contestability, not expertise worship; clear separation of fact-finding from values; public input mechanisms
