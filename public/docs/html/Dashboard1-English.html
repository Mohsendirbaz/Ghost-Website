Critical Insights

  • Bandwidth Bottleneck: LIDAR dominates at 2 GB/s. GPU reduces perception latency 7.5x (1455ms to 193ms).
  • Hardware Acceleration: TPU achieves 16.4x speedup vs CPU. Amdahl's Law limits: 90-95% parallel efficiency with serial overhead less than 5%.
  • Filter Selection: Kalman (2ms, real-time) for fusion. Particle (15ms, batch) for non-Gaussian. MPC (50ms, batch) for planning. Trade throughput vs. accuracy.
  • Nyquist Vulnerability: Radar Doppler aliasing at speeds over 150 mph. Side cameras at poor Nyquist margin. Multi-sensor fusion is mandatory.
  • Real-time Boundary: Filters below 8-10ms latency with less than 5% compute overhead enable closed-loop feedback. Above that: buffering required, planning decouples from sensing.
