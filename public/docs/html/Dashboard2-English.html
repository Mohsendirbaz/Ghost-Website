Critical System Constraints

  • • Latency Budget: Total ~300ms from sensor to actuator. At 100 km/h, vehicle travels 8.3m during processing—demands real-time OS and deterministic scheduling.
  • • Data Bandwidth: ~1.8 GB/s intra-vehicle traffic exceeds PCIe 3.0 capacity; requires PCIe 4.0+ and optimized data serialization (Protocol Buffers, ROS 2 DDS).
  • • Computational Load: ~600-1000 TFLOPS for real-time perception/prediction; demands multi-GPU systems (NVIDIA Drive Orin, Tesla FSD Computer) with power budgets of 500-1000W.
  • • Sensor Fusion: Clock synchronization across heterogeneous sensors is non-trivial; typical approaches use PPS (Pulse-Per-Second) sync with <10µs accuracy.
  • • Redundancy & Safety: Dual/triple sensor modalities required for SOTIF compliance. Fail-safe architecture mandates independent safety controllers capable of autonomous braking.
