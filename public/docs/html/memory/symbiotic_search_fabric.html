The Symbiotic Search Fabric

A mature architecture for co-adaptive · multi-coupled-tenant · client-owned · agent-operated · time-bounded search — in which the residue of agent experience is the raw material of the system's next design.
Search here is not a service that agents call; it is a habitat they inhabit, own under charter, and rebuild from the record of their own foraging. Warp threads are sovereign tenant estates; weft threads are governed coupling channels; agents are the shuttles; and the loom re-strings itself, one adjudicated generation at a time.

HOW TO READ THE FABRIC

1 · Follow blue down. A query enters the temporal contract spine as ⟨deadline, quality floor, budget⟩, clears an admission gate priced by the shadow auction, and descends only as deep into the strata as its deadline warrants. What cannot be answered within contract is refused aloud, with a counter-offer — never delivered silently late.

2 · Follow amber up. Every search — success or dead end — deposits a trace in its estate's append-only experience ledger. Ledger residue drains rightward into the distillery, where regret spectra and friction maps become the only legitimate fuel for design proposals.

3 · Follow green back. Proposals citing that experience pass twin trials, an adversarial gauntlet, and countersigned adjudication before compiling into generation G9 — a new frame, shipped with a rollback rail, that re-shapes the very strata, verbs, and prices the agents will forage under tomorrow. Co-adaptation is the point; the damper (loop gain < 1) is what keeps it from becoming resonance.

Kinship: the fabric deliberately imports the metabolic doctrine — residence-time stratification, shadow-priced recall, drift quarantine, honest refusal — and extends it from a single system's memory to a federation's shared habitat, where verification burden grows with a change's blast radius.
