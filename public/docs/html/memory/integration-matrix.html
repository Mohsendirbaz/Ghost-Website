Status of a link is not the status of its endpoints. WRITTEN means a source document already makes the binding; PROPOSED means the pairing is a defensible synthesis prepared for this atlas and carries a verification obligation; NOTIONAL means a speculative sketch that must not bear load. Node stamps mark evidence the other way: RUNS/MEASURED · SPECIFIED · NOTIONAL · RETIRED.

Color follows mechanism family, never rank: time & contracts · economy & coupling · algebraic laws · governance, evidence & instruments.
