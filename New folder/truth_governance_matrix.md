# Constitutional Truth-Governance Framework Matrix
## Mapping 63 Cases by Distance from Civic Discipline Core

**Compiled:** February 15, 2026  
**Purpose:** Systematic analysis of potential constitutional truth-governance frameworks, measuring their alignment with core civic epistemology principles

---

## Methodology

### Quantitative Measures (0-10 scale)

1. **Core Alignment** - Direct mapping to civic discipline of truth principles
   - 9-10: Direct instantiation of core epistemology
   - 7-8: Clear mapping with minimal translation
   - 5-6: Requires significant domain adaptation
   - 3-4: Loose analogy, substantial new principles needed
   - 1-2: Peripheral connection

2. **Safety Criticality** - Stakes if truth-governance fails
   - 9-10: Life/death, systemic collapse
   - 7-8: Major harm, irreversible damage
   - 5-6: Significant but recoverable harm
   - 3-4: Inconvenience, inefficiency
   - 1-2: Minimal direct harm

3. **Adversarial Pressure** - Forces actively corrupting truth
   - 9-10: Systematic, resourced, persistent adversaries
   - 7-8: Strong economic/political incentives to distort
   - 5-6: Moderate conflicts of interest
   - 3-4: Accidental drift, no active adversaries
   - 1-2: Benign environment

4. **Implementation Readiness** - Can we build this now?
   - 9-10: Immediate deployment possible
   - 7-8: Minor technical gaps
   - 5-6: Moderate development needed
   - 3-4: Significant research required
   - 1-2: Fundamental unknowns

5. **Human-AI Hybrid** - Degree of human-AI collaboration
   - 9-10: Deeply interleaved, continuous collaboration
   - 7-8: Human oversight of AI outputs
   - 5-6: Parallel human/AI pathways
   - 3-4: Mostly AI with human exception handling
   - 1-2: Pure AI or pure human

### Qualitative Classifications

- **Primary Principle**: Dominant civic epistemology principle (Provenance, Contestability, Correction, Independence, Warrant)
- **Separation Archetype**: Key institutional separation needed
- **Evidence Domain**: Primary type of warrant/evidence
- **Adaptation Challenge**: Main difficulty in maintaining truth over time

## CONCLUSION

The 63 cases reveal **three concentric circles** around the civic discipline of truth core:

**Inner Circle (Tier 1-2, 32 cases):** Direct instantiations and mappings where constitutional principles apply with minimal translation. These should be prioritized for immediate development.

**Middle Circle (Tier 3, 20 cases):** Substantive adaptations requiring domain-specific principles but maintaining clear epistemic foundations. These benefit from inner-circle learnings.

**Outer Circle (Tier 4, 11 cases):** Extended applications where truth-governance principles are loosely analogous. These may reveal novel constitutional requirements.

**Strategic Imperative:** Build from the core outward, using each tier to inform the next while maintaining constitutional coherence across domains.

The matrix reveals that **independence** (separation of epistemic powers) is the most frequently needed principle, appearing in 35% of cases. This suggests that constitutional architectures should emphasize institutional separation as the primary design pattern.

High-stakes applications cluster in healthcare, public safety, and democratic governance - domains where truth-governance failure has irreversible consequences. These deserve special attention and potentially stricter constitutional requirements.

The adversarial pressure distribution shows that information infrastructure, democratic processes, and financial systems face the strongest systematic corruption forces. These domains need the most robust constitutional protections and continuous adversarial testing.
