# Quality-Anchored Measure Theory: Integration Mapping

## Purpose

This document provides the **complete integration specification** for adding quality constraints to the measure-theoretic compositional framework. It maps quality operators $(Q, \Theta)$ into [Measure-Theoretic Multi-Scale Compositional Framework](Measure-Theoretic%20Multi-Scale%20Compositional%20Framew%202a05559a88b34fe3a843b86ce53ac63b.md), [Directional Perturbations in Adaptive Mode](Directional%20Perturbations%20in%20Adaptive%20Mode%20ed25d1258e2f48d090d321cfeb7f7a25.md), and [Extending Countable Additivity Beyond Parent-Child: Tone Thread Networks](Extending%20Countable%20Additivity%20Beyond%20Parent-Child%205697c4572f96475396d543a373d6d678.md).

**Design Philosophy**: Quality metrics provide **empirical anchors** that constrain the otherwise abstract measure space to configurations that satisfy validated linguistic quality standards.

**Integration Method**: Quality operators are added as **constraints** on admissible measure configurations, not as separate annotations.

---

## Core Quality Operators

### Quality Ensemble

**Definition**: A quality ensemble is a function $Q: (\Omega, \mathcal{F}_n, \mu_w, \{\mu_j\}) \to [0,1]^k$ that maps measure configurations to quality scores.

**Components**:

$$
Q(A) = \begin{bmatrix} Q_{\text{entity}}(A) \\ Q_{\text{toulmin}}(A) \\ Q_{\text{semantic}}(A) \\ Q_{\text{discourse}}(A) \\ Q_{\text{style}}(A) \\ Q_{\text{grammar}}(A) \end{bmatrix}
$$

where each $Q_i: \mathcal{F}_n \to [0,1]$ is a measurable quality metric.

**Quality Metrics** (from [](Untitled%20ef5b3d68ccdc475195379e44f2c5fdfb.md)):

- **Entity Grid Coherence**: $Q_{\text{entity}}(A)$ ∈ [0,1], measures entity continuity (validated ρ ≈ 0.68)
- **Toulmin Argument Strength**: $Q_{\text{toulmin}}(A)$ ∈ [0,1], measures claim-evidence-warrant structure
- **LSA Semantic Coherence**: $Q_{\text{semantic}}(A)$ ∈ [0,1], cosine similarity between adjacent segments (ρ ≈ 0.55)
- **RST Discourse Structure**: $Q_{\text{discourse}}(A)$ ∈ [0,1], rhetorical relations completeness
- **Readability/Style**: $Q_{\text{style}}(A)$ ∈ [0,1], formality and vocabulary appropriateness
- **Grammar**: $Q_{\text{grammar}}(A)$ ∈ [0,1], syntactic correctness

### Quality Thresholds

**Definition**: Minimum acceptable quality scores:

$$
\Theta = \begin{bmatrix} \theta_{\text{entity}} \\ \theta_{\text{toulmin}} \\ \theta_{\text{semantic}} \\ \theta_{\text{discourse}} \\ \theta_{\text{style}} \\ \theta_{\text{grammar}} \end{bmatrix} \in [0,1]^k
$$

**Typical Values** (to be calibrated empirically):

- $\theta_{\text{entity}} = 0.6$ (moderate entity continuity required)
- $\theta_{\text{toulmin}} = 0.5$ (basic argument structure required)
- $\theta_{\text{semantic}} = 0.4$ (loose semantic coherence required)
- $\theta_{\text{discourse}} = 0.5$ (basic discourse structure required)
- $\theta_{\text{style}} = 0.6$ (readability threshold)
- $\theta_{\text{grammar}} = 0.8$ (high grammatical correctness required)

**Context-Dependent Thresholds**: Different node types or tone types may have different $\Theta$.

### Admissibility Condition

**Definition**: A measure configuration $(\mu_w, \{\mu_j\})$ is **admissible** if and only if:

$$
Q_i(A) \geq \theta_i \quad \forall A \in \mathcal{F}_n, \forall i \in \{1, \ldots, k\}
$$

**Interpretation**: Every measurable set must meet all quality thresholds for the configuration to be valid.

A collection {μⱼ}ⱼ₌₁ᵐ of finite measures on (Ω, ℱ_n, μ_w, Q, Θ) satisfying:

1. Absolute continuity: μⱼ ≪ μ_w for all j

> 

> 
> 

> $$
> 

> Delta_A^Q(mu_w) = left{ mathbf{c} in Delta^{m-1} : Q(A, mathbf{c}, mu_w) geq Theta right}
> 

> $$
> 

> 
> 

> is the **quality-constrained simplex**.
> 

> 
> 

> **Proof**: By construction. The set $\Delta_A^Q$ is the subset of the compositional simplex satisfying quality constraints. If $\mathbf{c}^{\text{target}} \notin \Delta_A^Q$, then any tone measure system with composition $\mathbf{c}^{\text{target}}$ violates quality feasibility (constraint 4). > **Corollary 3.2 (Empty Feasible Simplex)**: For some nodes $A$ and word counts $\mu_w(A)$, the quality-constrained simplex may be empty: $\Delta_A^Q(\mu_w) = \emptyset$. In this case, no composition is quality-feasible at the given word count, and either:

> 

> 2. Quality thresholds must be relaxed: reduce $\Theta$

> 3. Quality override required: human decision with justification

## Integration Point 3: Quality-Constrained Composition Function

### Location in [Measure-Theoretic Multi-Scale Compositional Framework](Measure-Theoretic%20Multi-Scale%20Compositional%20Framew%202a05559a88b34fe3a843b86ce53ac63b.md)

**Section to Modify**: "Rewritten Core Definitions" → "Definition 4: Composition Function"

### Current Definition

```
Definition 4: Composition Function
For a measurable set A ∈ ℱ_n with μ_w(A) > 0, the composition vector is:

cₐ = [μ₁(A)/μ_w(A), ..., μₘ(A)/μ_w(A)]ᵀ

This is the Radon-Nikodym derivative cₐ = d{μⱼ}/dμ_w|_A
```

### Quality-Anchored Definition

**Replace with**:

```
Definition 4: Quality-Feasible Composition Function
For a measurable set A ∈ ℱ_n with μ_w(A) > 0, a composition vector

cₐ = [μ₁(A)/μ_w(A), ..., μₘ(A)/μ_w(A)]ᵀ

is quality-feasible if cₐ ∈ Δₐ^Q(μ_w(A)), where:

Δₐ^Q(μ_w) = {c ∈ Δ^(m-1) : Q(A, c, μ_w) ≥ Θ}

is the quality-constrained simplex at node A.

The composition is still the Radon-Nikodym derivative, but constrained:

cₐ = d{μⱼ}/dμ_w|_A  subject to  cₐ ∈ Δₐ^Q(μ_w(A))
```

**Add Definition**:

> **Definition 4.1 (Quality-Constrained Simplex)**: The quality-constrained simplex at node $A$ with word count $\mu_w$ is:
> 

> 
> 

> $$
> 

> Delta_A^Q(mu_w) = left{ mathbf{c} in mathbb{R}^m : c^{(j)} geq 0, sum_j c^{(j)} = 1, Q(A, mathbf{c}, mu_w) geq Theta right}
> 

> $$
> 

> 
> 

> **Properties**:
> 

> 1. $\Delta_A^Q(\mu_w) \subseteq \Delta^{m-1}$ (subset of standard simplex)
> 

> 2. $\Delta_A^Q$ is **word-count dependent**: as $\mu_w$ changes, the feasible region changes
> 

> 3. $\Delta_A^Q$ may be **empty**, **convex** (if quality metrics are convex functions), or **non-convex** (if quality metrics are non-convex)
> 

> 4. Boundary of $\Delta_A^Q$ represents **quality-criticality**: compositions at the boundary are barely admissible
>

**Add Helper Functions**:

```python
def project_onto_simplex(c_target, Δ_Q):
    """
    Project target composition onto quality-constrained simplex.
    
    Solve: argmin_{c ∈ Δ_Q} ||c - c_target||²
    
    Returns: c_projected if Δ_Q ≠ ∅, else None
    """
    if is_empty(Δ_Q):
        return None
    
    # Constrained quadratic optimization
    problem = QuadraticProgram(
        objective = lambda c: np.sum((c - c_target)**2),
        constraints = [
            lambda c: np.sum(c) == 1,  # Completeness
            lambda c: c >= 0,           # Non-negativity
            lambda c: Q(node, c, μ_w) >= Θ  # Quality
        ]
    )
    
    solution = solve_constrained_qp(problem)
    return solution.c if solution.status == 'optimal' else None

def estimate_min_words(node, c_target):
    """
    Estimate minimum word count for target composition to be quality-feasible.
    
    Binary search: find min μ_w such that Δ_node^Q(μ_w) ∩ B_ε(c_target) ≠ ∅
    where B_ε is ball of radius ε around c_target
    """
    μ_low, μ_high = w_mwt, 10 * current_μ_w
    
    while μ_high - μ_low > 1:
        μ_mid = (μ_low + μ_high) // 2
        if c_target in Δ_node^Q(μ_mid) or has_nearby_feasible(node, c_target, μ_mid, ε=0.1):
            μ_high = μ_mid
        else:
            μ_low = μ_mid
    
    return μ_high
```

## Quality-Preserving Conditional Expectation

### Standard Conditional Expectation

In the measure-theoretic framework, the conditional expectation of child composition given parent is:

$$
\mathbb{E}[c_{\mathcal{F}_k}^{(j)} \mid \mathcal{F}_{k-1}](x) = \frac{\mu_j(A_{k-1}(x))}{\mu_w(A_{k-1}(x))}
$$

where $`A_{k-1}(x)`$ is the parent node containing $`x`$.

This represents "expected child composition = parent composition".

### Quality-Constrained Conditional Expectation

With quality constraints, the conditional expectation must account for feasibility:

$$
\mathbb{E}^Q[c_{\mathcal{F}_k}^{(j)} \mid \mathcal{F}_{k-1}](x) = \arg\min_{\mathbf{c} \in \Delta_{child}^Q} \|\mathbf{c} - \mathbf{c}_{parent}\|^2
$$

This is the **closest quality-feasible composition** to the parent composition.

### Interpretation

**Case 1**: If $`\mathbf{c}_{parent} \in \Delta_{child}^Q`$, then:

$$
\mathbb{E}^Q[\mathbf{c} \mid parent] = \mathbf{c}_{parent}
$$

Child can inherit parent composition without quality violation.

**Case 2**: If $`\mathbf{c}_{parent} \notin \Delta_{child}^Q`$, then:

$$
\mathbb{E}^Q[\mathbf{c} \mid parent] = \text{projection of } \mathbf{c}_{parent} \text{ onto } \Delta_{child}^Q
$$

Child cannot inherit parent composition—nearest feasible composition is expected value.

### Quality-Decomposed Vertical Shear

**Definition**: The vertical shear now decomposes into forced and voluntary components:

$$
\Delta_k c^{(j)} = c_{\mathcal{F}_k}^{(j)} - \mathbb{E}[c_{\mathcal{F}_k}^{(j)} \mid \mathcal{F}_{k-1}]
$$

With quality:

$$
\Delta_k^Q c^{(j)} = \underbrace{\mathbb{E}^Q[c \mid parent] - c_{parent}}_{\text{forced shear (quality)}} + \underbrace{c_{child} - \mathbb{E}^Q[c \mid parent]}_{\text{voluntary shear (specialization)}}
$$

**Interpretation**:
- **Forced shear**: Deviation caused by quality constraints (parent composition infeasible for child)
- **Voluntary shear**: Intentional specialization beyond what quality requires

**Algorithm: Diagnose Vertical Shear**:

```

def diagnose_vertical_shear(child: Node, parent: Node, Q: QualityEnsemble, Θ: Dict):

"""

Decompose vertical shear into forced (quality) and voluntary (specialization) components.

"""

c_child = child.composition

c_parent = parent.composition

shear_total = c_child - c_parent

# Check if parent composition is feasible for child

if c_parent in Δ_child^Q(child.word_count):

# Parent composition is feasible - all shear is voluntary

return ShearDiagnosis(

type='voluntary_specialization',

forced_component=np.zeros_like(c_parent),

voluntary_component=shear_total,

magnitude=np.linalg.norm(shear_total),

interpretation='Child intentionally specialized from parent'

)

else:

# Parent composition violates quality at child scale

# Find closest feasible composition

c_closest = project_onto_simplex(

c_parent,

Δ_child^Q(child.word_count)

)

if c_closest is None:

# Empty feasible simplex

return ShearDiagnosis(

type='quality_infeasible',

interpretation='No quality-feasible composition exists for child',

requires_human=True

)

# Decompose shear

shear_forced = c_closest - c_parent

shear_voluntary = c_child - c_closest

return ShearDiagnosis(

type='quality_constrained',

forced_component=shear_forced,

voluntary_component=shear_voluntary,

forced_magnitude=np.linalg.norm(shear_forced),

voluntary_magnitude=np.linalg.norm(shear_voluntary),

interpretation=f'Shear has {100*np.linalg.norm(shear_forced)/np.linalg.norm(shear_total):.1f}% forced by quality'

)

Given parent P ∈ ℱ_{k-1} with children {C₁, ..., Cₘ} ⊂ ℱ_k, a quality-feasible disintegration is a collection of conditional measures {μ_w^P(C_i)} such that:

1. Conservation: Σᵢ μ_w^P(C_i) = μ_w(P)

2. Non-negativity: μ_w^P(C_i) ≥ w_mwt for all i

3. Quality feasibility: For each child C_i with target composition c_Cᵢ^target:
   
   Q(C_i, c_Cᵢ^target, μ_w^P(C_i)) ≥ Θ
   
   where μ_j^P(C_i) = c_Cᵢ^(j),target · μ_w^P(C_i)

4. Parent tone conservation: Σᵢ μ_j^P(C_i) = μ_j(P) for all tones j

**Existence**: A quality-feasible disintegration exists if and only if the system of constraints (1)-(4) has a solution. This is a constrained optimization problem, not a simple linear allocation.

**Non-Uniqueness**: Unlike standard disintegration (which is unique), quality-feasible disintegration may have multiple solutions or no solution.
```

**Add Algorithm**:

```
Algorithm: Quality-Feasible Disintegration Solver

Input:
- Parent P with μ_w(P), {μ_j(P)}
- Children {C₁, ..., Cₘ}
- Target compositions {c_Cᵢ^target}
- Quality ensemble Q, thresholds Θ

Output:
- Feasible allocation {α_i} with μ_w(C_i) = α_i · μ_w(P)
- OR infeasibility report

Formulation:

minimize: Σᵢ ||c_Cᵢ^achieved - c_Cᵢ^target||²

subject to:
- Σᵢ α_i = 1                          [conservation]
- α_i ≥ w_mwt / μ_w(P)                [minimum threshold]
- Q(C_i, c_Cᵢ, α_i · μ_w(P)) ≥ Θ      [quality feasibility]
- Σᵢ c_Cᵢ^(j) · α_i = c_P^(j)         [parent tone conservation]

where c_Cᵢ^achieved are the decision variables (achieved compositions)
and α_i are derived from c_Cᵢ^achieved and parent tone conservation.

Solver:
1. Initialize with proportional allocation: α_i = 1/m
2. Iterate:
   a. For each child, project c_Cᵢ^target onto Δ_Cᵢ^Q(α_i · μ_w(P))
   b. Check parent tone conservation
   c. Adjust α_i to satisfy conservation
   d. Repeat until convergence or max iterations
3. If no feasible solution found, return infeasibility report with options:
   - Increase parent word count
   - Relax target compositions
   - Relax quality thresholds
   - Merge children to increase individual word counts
```

## Quality-Constrained Optimization Framework

### Objective Function

The quality-anchored framework extends the compositional optimization problem to include quality as both constraint and objective:

$$
\min_{\{\mu_w, \{\mu_j\}\}} \left[ \sum_{A \in \mathcal{F}_k} \|\mathbf{c}_A - \mathbf{c}_A^{\text{target}}\|^2 + \lambda_{\tau} \sum_A \|\boldsymbol{\tau}_A\|^2 + \lambda_Q \sum_A \|Q(A) - Q_{\text{target}}\|^2 \right]
$$

where:
- First term: Minimize compositional deviation from targets
- Second term: Minimize vertical/horizontal shear (compositional smoothness)
- Third term: Minimize quality deviation from targets (NEW)

### Constraints

$$
\begin{align}
&\sum_{C \subset P} \mu_w(C) = \mu_w(P) && \text{(parent-child conservation)} \\
&\sum_j \mu_j(A) = \mu_w(A) && \text{(completeness)} \\
&\mu_w(S) \geq w_{\text{mwt}} && \text{(minimum threshold)} \\
&Q_i(A) \geq \theta_i && \text{(quality thresholds - HARD constraint)} \\
&\mathbf{c}_A \in \Delta_A^Q(\mu_w(A)) && \text{(feasibility)}
\end{align}
$$

### Multi-Objective Formulation

Alternatively, treat as multi-objective optimization:

$$
\min_{\{\mu_w, \{\mu_j\}\}} \begin{bmatrix} f_1(\mu) \\ f_2(\mu) \\ f_3(\mu) \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \text{Compositional Error} \\ \text{Shear Magnitude} \\ \text{Quality Deviation} \end{bmatrix}
$$

Find Pareto-optimal solutions: configurations where improving one objective degrades another.

**Interpretation**: There is typically no single optimal solution—composition, smoothness, and quality trade off. The Pareto frontier reveals feasible trade-offs, and human judgment selects among them.

### Solver Strategy

**Two-Stage Approach**:

1. **Stage 1: Quality-Feasible Region**: Find any measure configuration satisfying quality constraints
   
   $$
   \text{find } (\mu_w, \{\mu_j\}) \text{ such that } Q_i(A) \geq \theta_i \, \forall A, i
   $$
   
   If no solution exists, relax constraints or report infeasibility.

2. **Stage 2: Optimize Within Feasible Region**: Given quality-feasible starting point, optimize composition and smoothness:
   
   $$
   \min \|\mathbf{c} - \mathbf{c}^{\text{target}}\|^2 + \lambda \|\boldsymbol{\tau}\|^2 \quad \text{subject to quality constraints}
   $$
