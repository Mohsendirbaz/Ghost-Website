“The Apple Does Not Fall Far From Its Tree”**
The 4th of July is definitely a cause for celebration—for it has become home to the troubled men of plenty. The root of our apple tree is the early composition of America, when it was only comprised of a few hundred thousand soles. The fallen apple is today’s America.
History offers no moment of repentance, no cleansing pivot. From the first ship to the latest jet, America has never purged itself of the trouble it imported. Deportations may exist everywhere, but in America, the scoundrel was never sent home—he was handed a farm, a vote, or later, a corporate charter. No swiss engineer of some status turned to his kid and said lets go discover the new world, If history had a witness protection program, it was the Atlantic.
Whether by gunpowder or paperwork, by saloon stickups or spreadsheet manipulations, America’s heartbeat has always been the same:

**a republic of tricksters and profiteers balanced upon the bent backs of the enduring majority.**
