- *A Quivering Teen**
Quivering - not from fear, but from the cold and something deeper. A teenager, maybe fifteen, maybe seventeen, it's hard to tell when survival ages you faster than years do.
The shaking is constant now, a rhythm that matches the shivers running through thin shoulders. There's no heavy coat, just layers that don't quite add up to warmth. Hands raw and red, clasped around something precious and terrible.
The eyes don't meet passersby. There's a weight there, the kind that makes you understand some stories don't have happy middles, only befores and afters.
