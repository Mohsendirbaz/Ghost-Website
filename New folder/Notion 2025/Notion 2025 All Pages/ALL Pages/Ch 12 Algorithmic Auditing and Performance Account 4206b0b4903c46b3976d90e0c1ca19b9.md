- *CHAPTER 12: Algorithmic Auditing and Performance Accountability**
**Content Summary:**
Establishes enforceable accountability frameworks requiring AI systems to demonstrate rather than merely claim benefits. **Frames algorithmic bias as a leadership and governance failure, not merely a technical problem.** Uses decades of lending discrimination (Countrywide $335M, SunTrust $21M, Ally $98M settlements) to show how America's weak regulatory frameworks and institutional absence allowed bias to persist. Covers transparency requirements, third-party auditing, metrics standardization, and consequences for failures. **Demonstrates why technical solutions require governance foundations that America has systematically failed to build.** Parallel to climate book's "Performance Accountability."
