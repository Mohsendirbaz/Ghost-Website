- *What We Know Without Being Told**
These shoes aren't being sold because they were outgrown. Babies outgrow shoes, but parents keep them - in boxes, in closets, in memory chests. First shoes are sacred objects, markers of milestones.
When you sell a baby's barely-worn shoes on a freezing street corner, you're not decluttering. You're surviving.
The questions pile up like snow:
- Was this the teen's younger sibling?
- Their own child?
- A baby they were caring for?
- What happened that these shoes are now currency instead of keepsake?
- How many times did they consider this before the cold made the choice for them?
There's a mathematics to desperation. When you calculate what you can bear to lose against what you need to survive, sometimes even the unbearable becomes negotiable.
Douglas Street has seen this calculation before. It will see it again.
