- *CHAPTER 28: Can Democratic Societies Build Accountable AI?**
**Content Summary:**
Final synthesis chapter posing the central question: Can democracies build AI systems accountable to publics rather than concentrated corporate power? **Argues why America is disqualified from leading global AI ethics efforts** due to: (1) Trump's 80-year family pattern of truth manipulation exacerbating (2) America's historic institutional absence and (3) systematically weak regulatory frameworks. Examines whether political will and institutional capacity exist when leadership itself is compromised. Makes final moral/political case that **technical problems (bias, transparency) are fundamentally governance and leadership failures**. Parallel to climate book's "Prosecuting Inequity - The Path Forward."
