# Customize Notion for your team

Notion’s projects and tasks are built using **databases**, which means you can customize them to your team’s needs.

- 📹 [**Organize information with databases](https://www.notion.so/help/notion-academy/course/organizing-information-databases):** Watch video tutorials on how databases work, creating timeline and board/kanban views, filtering projects and tasks, and more
    
    [Notion Academy — Organize information with databases](https://www.notion.so/help/notion-academy/course/organizing-information-databases)
    
- 🌟 [**Explore project management integrations](https://www.notion.so/integrations/all):** Notion can be used with many other tools. Explore integrations with tools like Dropbox, GitHub, Figma, and more.
    - If your team uses **forms**, you can try one of the form integrations built for Notion
    
    [Integrations – Notion](https://www.notion.so/integrations/all)
